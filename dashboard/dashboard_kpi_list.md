# AtmoSync Dashboard KPI List

## 1. Climate Monitoring
- Total sensors monitored: 9
- Average temperature (°C)
- Average relative humidity (%)
- Temperature trends over time
- Humidity trends over time
- Sensor-wise temperature and humidity comparison
- Missing readings and data quality

## 2. Cold-Chain Monitoring
- Product-simulating temperature (TP)
- Temperature stability over time
- Potential temperature-exposure risk
- Refrigerator zone-wise temperature comparison

Note: Household refrigerator data is a separate dataset.

## 3. Market Analytics
- Commodity price trends
- Monthly price changes
- Commodity price volatility
- Climate and market trend comparison

Note: Global commodity prices alone cannot establish actual arbitrage profit.
